title: Axon Robotics
---

**Axon Robotics** is an FTC parts supplier known for their high-performance programmable servos. Their flagship products — the Axon MAX and Axon Mini offer significantly more torque and speed than stock REV or goBILDA servos, with built-in programmable features like adjustable **speed profiles** and **position feedback**. Teams use Axon servos for high-demand mechanisms like active **intakes**, wrist **joints**, and claw **pivots** where a standard servo would **stall** or be too slow. They've become increasingly popular among competitive teams looking for a servo **upgrade** without going full custom. To buy your own, go to [axon-robotics.com](https://axon-robotics.com/)
