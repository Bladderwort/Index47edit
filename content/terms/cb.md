[Cookbook](https://cookbook.dairy.foundation) is a user guide for FTC students developed by the Dairy Foundation. It acts as a "clean, quick reference for common issues faced in the context of the FIRST Tech Challenge." This includes common bugs with pathing libraries, and general knowledge that can be helpful for FTC. Similar to Index47, cookbook is open sourced and can be contributed by anyone! Visit the [repository](https://github.com/dr-hextanium/cookbook) on how you can get started with this.
