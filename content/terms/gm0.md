**Game Manual 0** is a comprehensive **guide** for FTC teams from all around the world. It covers multiple aspects of FTC, explaining ways to organize robotics teams, and also covers topics such as **design skills**, general advice for **Hardware Components**, **Manufacturing**, **Electrical tips** and **Software Advice**. It is a useful tool for many teams, especially newer ones and assists them through the process of building a robot.
