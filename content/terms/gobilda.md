title: goBILDA
---

**goBILDA** is an FTC parts supplier known for its robust channel-based build system using metric hardware and 8mm REX shafts on a consistent 48mm grid pattern. Their product line includes the popular [Yellow Jacket motors](https://www.gobilda.com/yellow-jacket-planetary-gear-motors/), **Super Strafer chassis** kits, **linear slides** (Viper Slides), **servos**, and a wide range of structural and motion components. goBILDA parts are designed to **work together** seamlessly, which makes building mechanisms faster. Many competitive FTC teams rely on goBILDA as their **primary** build system because of the high part density and **strong community support**. To see the full library visit [gobilda.com](https://www.gobilda.com/)
