---
title: Inspire Award
---

**Inspire Award** is the highest award a team can receive at an FTC event. It goes to the team that best embodies the full [FIRST](https://www.firstinspires.org/) experience which normally includes excellence in outreach, robot game, and gracious professionalism. Introduced in the DECODE season, the inspire award can give 60 ranking points which often results in guaranteed advancement!
