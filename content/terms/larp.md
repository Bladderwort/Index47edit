aliases: [Larp]

__Live Action Role Play__ commonly known as "larp" is a bad action, regularly committed by FTC students. It means that a student is trying to __act__ like someone that they are not, or act like they are __superior__ in some way when they are not. Example: __I am part Exodus 30030.__ This would be __factually incorrect__ and trying to present yourself __higher__ as they won the FIRST __world__ __championship__ for the DECODE season. Larping can have many __consequences__ such as being socially isolated where no one will want to talk or communicate with you. Visit [urbandictionary.com](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=LARP) for more details
