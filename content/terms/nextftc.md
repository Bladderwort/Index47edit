**NextFTC** is a collection of command based libraries developed by Rowan McAlpin, Davis Luxenberg, and Zach Harel. NextBindings provides complex gamepad integration, and NextControl has **PID/PIDF control**, **feedforward**, **motion profiling**, and **Kalman filters**. NextFTC is written in **Kotlin** with **Java** support, and has extensions to work with **PedroPathing** and **RoadRunner**. More information and documentation can be viewed at [nextftc.dev](https://nextftc.dev/).
