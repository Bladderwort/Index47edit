**Scouting** in FTC is a process of collecting and analyzing data on other teams at your competition. This is for your team to be able to make a decision in alliance selection. Even if you have never been a captain before it is always good to be ready for whatever happens. It generally has two components: match scouting, where scouts track each team's performance across qualification matches by recording things like auto points, teleop cycles, endgame success, and penalties; and pit scouting, where team members visit other teams' pits to ask directly about robot capabilities and autonomous options. All of this data is compiled and used to identify which teams would best complement your robot going into playoffs, and to avoid being caught off guard by an alliance partner or opponent you know nothing about.
