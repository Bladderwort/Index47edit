---
title: SolversLib
---

**SolversLib** is an updated and maintained fork of the older FTCLib, created by FTC Team **23511**, Seattle Solvers. One of SolversLib's modern features is easy integration with **Pedro Pathing**, and recently an integration with **Photon** which helps with quick **loop times**! Check out the documentation at [docs.seattlesolvers.com](https://docs.seattlesolvers.com/).
