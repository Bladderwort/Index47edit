---
title: Swerve Drive
aliases: [Swerve]
---

__Swerve__ is a drivetrain type that has 4 wheels, each moving in an __independent__ direction, this means that all movements are tangent, often resulting is much faster acceleration than mecanum drive. Placing these wheels in an __X shape__ will allow for the robot to be much harder to move, as there is opposing friction on the ground.
